About Edana Montessori School

A caring community with big ambitions

A BRIEF HISTORY OF EDANA MONTESSORI SCHOOL Edana Montessori School was established on 14th September, 2009, through the vision, passion, and commitment of its founder, Mrs Uchechi Ann Alozie (Director 1), alongside Mrs Edith Ogechi Akinkunmi (Director 2). The name EDANA was creatively formed from the names of two sisters, Edith and Ann, who are shareholders in the school. The name represents the founders' vision of building an educational institution committed to nurturing young minds, promoting academic excellence, and laying a solid foundation for children's future success. The school began its journey with just five children: one of the founder's daughters, one child of a staff member, and three other children from the same parent. Although the beginning was humble, the vision was great. With determination, dedication, and a passion for quality education, the founders embarked on the journey of building a school that would make a lasting impact on children's lives. On 19th May, 2011, Edana Montessori School was officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), marking a significant milestone in the school's history and development.
